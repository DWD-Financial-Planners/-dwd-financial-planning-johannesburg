## 🚨 Warning Signs When Choosing a Financial Planner

Not all financial advisors operate with long-term strategy in mind.

Be cautious if you experience:

❌ Product recommendations before a full financial review  
❌ Unclear or hidden fee structures  
❌ No scheduled annual reviews  
❌ One-size-fits-all retirement annuity advice  
❌ No discussion of risk tolerance or life changes  

Financial planning should evolve with your life.

---

## ✅ Questions You Should Ask Before You Commit

Before selecting a financial planner, ask questions that reveal depth and experience.

✔ How will my retirement fund support my lifestyle at retirement?  
✔ What happens to my investments during market downturns?  
✔ How is my life cover structured to protect my family?  
✔ How often will my portfolio be reviewed and adjusted?  
✔ How will my estate plan reduce delays and unnecessary costs?  

A confident advisor welcomes informed clients.
